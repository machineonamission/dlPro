Welcome to dlPro!

How to use:

  1. Navigate to the page you want to download

  2. Click the dlPro icon (it may be under the "extensions" button that looks like a puzzle piece)

    Pro-tip: click the "pin" 📌 icon to make the dlPro icon always visible at the top!

  3. Select your download preset and click confirm
